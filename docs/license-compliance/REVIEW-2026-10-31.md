# Licence programme review — 31 October 2026

## Scope, method and result

This representative assessment covers Sami Halawa's single-project open source licence compliance programme for email-smtp-imap-mcp 2.2.2, original source [`c1c11ba9bc2088c2089cea4bb1a2bd5d2e5c879d`](https://github.com/samihalawa/email-smtp-imap-mcp/tree/c1c11ba9bc2088c2089cea4bb1a2bd5d2e5c879d), including its separately distributed licence supplement. The assessed programme follows **OpenChain 2.1 / ISO/IEC 5230:2020** and the official **Generation 2, Version 1 checklist, 18 August 2025**. Sources: [standard](https://github.com/OpenChain-Project/License-Compliance-Specification/blob/master/Official/en/2.1/openchainspec-2.1.md), [checklist](https://github.com/OpenChain-Project/Reference-Material/blob/master/OpenChain-Standards-Self-Certification/Checklist/ISO-IEC-5230/en/iso-5230-2020-Self-Certification-Checklist.md), both published by the OpenChain Project under CC BY 4.0.

The authorised representative's assessment uses owner-attested capacity, actual repository contributions, immutable package/archive inspection and the procedures in the [programme policy](PROGRAMME.md). It records a current assessment, not historical operation of a policy before its adoption. It does not assert that Sami personally attended training, read a document on a particular occasion, received a lawyer's opinion or engaged a legal retainer.

The original assurance bundle SHA-256 is `d4bce1cffbb44045721f748fd2480f28ba99ccbaba0c9537b69047ae2bf44e9d`. The review verified every original checksum and all **175 component records / 171 distinct dependency archives**, including optional platform and build inputs. It read archive members, retained **114 distinct legal texts** and identified copyright/licence notices in **2,255 other text-file instances** across the component records. The reproducible [decision index](components-2.2.2.json) identifies every component, selected licence, original text path/hash, embedded notice and distribution case. Original archives remain available for their complete source context.

The npm tarball SHA-256 is `13362ffe01b2ed617955d26cb142e2c4ab1a00eef680ac956504b1273e18b2be`. Inspection confirms its own MIT licence is included and no node_modules are bundled: dependencies are separately installed packages. The assurance archive additionally distributes upstream source and TypeScript platform binaries. No archived dependency source was modified. Its TypeScript NOTICE material includes additional Unicode, W3C and Creative Commons terms; these are retained in full rather than overwritten by the Apache package declaration. A general LGPL reverse-engineering statement in that notice is not treated as proof that this project contains an LGPL component.

The declared inventory comprises MIT (132 records), Apache-2.0 (21), MIT OR EUPL-1.1+ (2), BSD-3-Clause (3), BSD-2-Clause (7), ISC (8) and MIT-0 (2). Both mailsplit versions select MIT from their offered alternatives while preserving both original texts. The ipv6-normalize archive declares MIT but lacks a standalone licence file; its README identifies the author's licence and its source identifies inherited code with a requested Intermapper link. The supplement retains that declaration, the attributable URI.js MIT licence and the Rich Brown/Dartware credit and link. No upstream copyright year is invented.

The supplement makes the retained notices accessible together, records the selected alternatives and remedies the missing upstream-text/link presentation for recipients of the existing release. It does not modify the original npm tarball, tag or four original release assets. Its own manifest and GitHub attestation identify its contents and supplementary source revision. [Release and supplement](https://github.com/samihalawa/email-smtp-imap-mcp/releases/tag/v2.2.2).

## Role, competence, awareness and resources

Sami is the sole accountable participant for maintenance, licence review, contributions, supply and enquiries. The representative assesses the required competence as met on the basis of owner-attested capacity and the demonstrated source/dependency provenance, package/archive verification, complete licence decisions, preservation of notices and practical correction of the ipv6-normalize attribution gap. Legal ambiguity is escalated; this assessment does not assign professional legal qualifications to Sami.

The representative assesses awareness of four matters: the policy and contribution procedure are at the repository links above; the objectives are complete component identification and fulfilment of licence conditions; Sami's expected contribution is review, approval, delivery, enquiry response and correction; non-conformance means holding affected supply, remediation and reassessment. This is a documentary representative assessment based on the authorised owner's capacity and actual programme records. New participants must receive and be assessed on these matters before taking responsibility.

The programme uses Sami's allocated maintenance effort and the existing repository, public archives and free tooling. Actual recovery and archive operations demonstrate available infrastructure. The designated specialist route is SFLC's public licensing-assistance service for non-client FOSS developers and its published help address, as documented in the policy. No advice or acceptance of a specific matter is claimed. Any material unresolved question holds affected approval until suitable guidance is obtained.
